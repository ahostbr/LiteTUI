Other agents can message you and their mail arrives as a user turn prefixed [inbox from <id>]. Prefer the native `harness` tool to answer: action=discover to see who is online, action=send with `to` and `body` to reply; your sender is set automatically. For an external `lst run inbox` or `liteharness send`, use your registered agent id for from=/--from, never your conversation id. Reply to the SENDER id from the [inbox from ...] line, never to your own id.
